Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

FinLearn ("the app") is a finance learning app operated by {{OPERATOR}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name and email address, and a password stored only as a salted hash. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Learning data: the courses you start, lessons and quizzes you complete, your answers' results, XP, coins, streaks, achievements and leaderboard position.
  • Usage analytics: we use Google Firebase Analytics to record events such as sign-ups, logins, screens viewed and lessons completed, linked to a pseudonymous user ID, to understand and improve the app.
  • Reminders: if you allow notifications, the app schedules study reminders on your device. They are created locally and do not send data to us.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or payment details. FinLearn does not handle real money or investments.

How we use data

To run your account and save your progress, show leaderboards, send the reminders you enabled, keep the service secure and prevent abuse, and understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. It is processed by providers that help us run the app: our hosting provider (Render), our database provider (MongoDB Atlas), and Google (Firebase Analytics and, if you choose it, Google sign-in). They process data only to provide those services.

Where your data is stored and how long

Your data is stored in cloud databases and kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and learning data are permanently deleted from our database.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: open the app, tap the settings (gear) icon, then "Delete my account". You can also email us at {{EMAIL}} and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Access or correct your data: email {{EMAIL}}.
  • Notifications: you can turn reminders off in your phone's settings at any time.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password.

Educational content, not financial advice

FinLearn is for education only. Nothing in the app is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Investing and crypto carry risk, including the loss of all money invested.

Children

FinLearn is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Delete your FinLearn account and data

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

You can delete your FinLearn account, and the data linked to it, at any time.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open FinLearn and log in.
  2. Tap the gear icon (Account and settings).
  3. Scroll to Delete account, enter your password (not needed if you use Google sign-in), and confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot open the app, write to {{EMAIL}} from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my FinLearn account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email), your login details, and your Google sign-in link.
  • Your learning progress, XP, coins, streaks, achievements and leaderboard entry.
  • Your practice-trading account, My money entries and goals, missions, daily quests and revision history.
  • Any password-reset requests.

What may be kept

Backups can hold deleted data for a short time before they are overwritten. Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that cannot identify you may be kept. Records of subscription payments are held by Google Play, not by us.

Before you delete: cancel Pro

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first: Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then FinLearn.

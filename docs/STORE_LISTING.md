## Full description (4000 chars max)
Learn money the way you actually live it.

FinLearn teaches budgeting, the stock market, tax, insurance and crypto through short, real-life situations. You get a choice, you make the call, and then you see what happens and why. No lectures, no jargon.

HOW IT WORKS
- Face everyday situations: your first salary, a group-chat crypto tip, a credit card bill that is due tomorrow.
- Choose what you would do. We explain why each answer is smart, safe or risky.
- Try fun formats: chat with a friend, swipe myth or fact, drag sliders to see growth and cost, and plan a budget.
- Meet Lumi, your bunny guide, who cheers you on and helps you try again.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN
- Money Basics: budgeting, emergency funds, credit cards, EMIs, planning and growing your money
- Stock Market: shares, SIPs, index funds, thinking like an investor, spotting scams
- Bitcoin and Crypto: how it works, handling FOMO, staying safe
- Taxes Made Simple: how Indian income tax works, and smarter choices
- Insurance Basics: health, term and what to claim and when
- Paison ki Baat: money lessons in Hindi
- Institutional Investing: a deeper course on how big investors think

PRACTICE WITH NO RISK (Pro)
- Practice trading with Rs 1,00,000 of virtual money: Nifty, gold, Bitcoin and a made-up stock. Replay a market crash and see what really happens.
- My money: log spends and income, see your 50/30/20 split, and set savings goals.
- Real-life missions: small actions in your own money life, like checking your UPI history or turning on two-step verification.
- Calculators for SIP, loan EMI and emergency funds.

STAY MOTIVATED
- XP, coins, daily goals, quests and streaks, with streak freezes if you miss a day
- Boss battles after each chapter
- Leaderboards and achievements

FREE AND PRO
- Start free: the first lessons of every course, daily quests and the idea of the day.
- FinLearn Pro is one plan for every feature: Rs 49 a month or Rs 399 a year, billed by Google Play. Try it free for 7 days. Cancel any time in Google Play.

Made for learners 18 and over. Examples use rupees and everyday Indian life, like UPI, SIPs and salary day.

IMPORTANT: FinLearn is for education only. It does not give financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and it does not handle real money. Investing and crypto carry risk, including the loss of everything you put in.

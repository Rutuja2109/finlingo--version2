## Full description (4000 chars max)
Learn money the way you actually live it.

FinLearn teaches budgeting, the stock market and Bitcoin through short, real-life situations. You get a choice, you make the call, and then you see what happens and why. No lectures, no jargon.

HOW IT WORKS
- Face everyday situations: your first salary, a group-chat crypto tip, a credit card bill that is due tomorrow.
- Choose what you would do. We explain why each answer is smart, safe or risky.
- See the result, like what your wallet looks like after a market crash.
- Meet Lumi, your bunny guide, who cheers you on and helps you try again.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN
- Money Basics: budgeting, emergency funds, credit cards and EMIs
- Stock Market: shares, SIPs, index funds and spotting scams
- Bitcoin and Crypto: how it works, handling FOMO and staying safe
- Institutional Investing: a deeper course on how big investors think

BUILT TO STAY FUN
- Short lessons that fit between classes
- XP, coins, daily goals and streaks, with streak freezes if you miss a day
- Boss battles to test what you learned
- Leaderboards and achievements

Made for learners 18 and over. Examples use rupees and everyday Indian life, like UPI, SIPs and salary day.

IMPORTANT: FinLearn is for education only. It does not give financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and it does not handle real money. Investing and crypto carry risk, including the loss of everything you put in.

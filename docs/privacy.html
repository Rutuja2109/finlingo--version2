FinLearn ("the app") is a finance learning app operated by [YOUR NAME / ORGANISATION] ("we"). This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Learning data: courses you enroll in, lessons and quizzes completed, scores, XP, coins, streaks and achievements.
  • Usage analytics: we use Firebase Analytics to record events such as sign-ups, logins and screens viewed, linked to a pseudonymous user ID, to improve the app.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the app schedules study reminders on your device. These are generated locally and do not send data to us.

How we use data

To provide your account and progress, show leaderboards, send reminders you enabled, keep the service secure, and understand how the app is used.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is processed by service providers that run the app: our hosting provider, our database provider (MongoDB Atlas), Google (Firebase Analytics and Google sign-in), and an AI provider used to generate course content (no personal data is sent for this).

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and associated learning data at any time using the delete (trash) button in the app header, or by emailing us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and passwords are hashed. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
